BRANCH-AND-BOUND======================================================================Consider the offline corpus as a searchable hierarchy/DAG.A query first produces a high-recall candidate set.Very cheap models then estimate something resembling:    P(useful evidence for question Q exists inside corpus node N)Early stages intentionally use LOW thresholds and preserve many branches.Only branches that continue to appear useful receive additional compute.Example conceptual flow:    QUESTION        ↓    deterministic high-recall candidate generation    BM25 / title lookup / aliases / entities / static embeddings        ↓    hundreds of candidate corpus nodes        ↓    tiny relevance model    HIGH RECALL    LOW threshold        ↓    surviving articles / sections        ↓    somewhat stronger relevance model        ↓    surviving sections / paragraphs        ↓    fine relevance / evidence extraction        ↓    exact sentences / spans / facts        ↓    evidence packet    approximately 300–800 tokens        ↓    ONE small generative LLM        ↓    cited answerFalse positives early in the cascade cost compute.False negatives can destroy the answer.Therefore early stages should be optimized heavily for recall and especiallyfor avoiding catastrophic pruning.======================================================================IMPORTANT CURRENT MODEL FAMILIES TO to this research.Do not treat arbitrary reranker logits as calibrated probabilities.For every relevance model investigate:    calibration    threshold behavior    precision-recall    false-negative SURVIVAL======================================================================This is a critical metric.If required evidence survives each pruning stage with probability r, thenmultiple stages required-evidence survival after Stage 2    required-evidence survival after Stage 3    final evidence recallRecord EXACTLY where required evidence is lost.Do not report only final NDCG or aggregate retrieval score.A cascade that ranks beautifully but destroys the sole answering passage isa failure.======================================================================TRAINING
